01 / BACKGROUND

Academic
profile.

Yuxing Mao teaches and conducts research in computer networks, image processing, and artificial intelligence. His work includes infrared imaging, pattern recognition, power Internet of Things, and big-data analysis.

He is a senior member of the China Society of Image and Graphics. His profile also lists a visiting-scholar affiliation with Concordia University's Centre for Pattern Recognition and Machine Intelligence (CENPARMI), Canada.
